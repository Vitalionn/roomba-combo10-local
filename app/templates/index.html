Importing rooms: release the bridge (button below), start a run in the iRobot app with some of the rooms ticked (with all rooms ticked the app sends no room list), swipe the iRobot app away without stopping (stopping overwrites the command), wait until the bridge is connected again, then and then click Dock here. Repeat with the remaining rooms; the lists are merged.

Allow the iRobot app (capture commands)

The robot accepts one local connection. Release the bridge for 10 minutes, do in the iRobot app what you want to capture (dock buttons, rooms), and the bridge then logs the new command under Events as "appCommand". For rooms, click "import rooms" afterwards.
